Block Bomb — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Block Bomb ("the game", "we") uses. We do not run our own servers and we do not sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your scores, statistics, settings, power-ups and the game in progress are saved on your device. You can remove them at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your game progress (best score, statistics, daily streak and power-ups) is saved to your Google Play Games account so you can restore it on another device. Your best score is submitted to the game's leaderboard, where other players may see your Play Games name and avatar. This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and general app usage to show ads and measure their performance. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice later. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. We receive only confirmation that the purchase was made; we never see your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
